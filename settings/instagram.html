Manage Instagram

Keep the parts of Instagram you need. Choose what you'd rather avoid.

If you've blocked Instagram entirely in FocusUp, these settings won't override that restriction.

What can you access?

Choose 1 to stop after the first Reel, or a number up to 20. The limit applies when you choose “Stop after a few Reels.” Leaving Reels, switching modes, or reloading starts a new visit. If a Reel can't be identified, it stays blocked while a limit is active.

Keep Instagram distraction-free

Messaging stays accessible. Reels opened on a separate Reel page follow your Reel settings. Videos played inside a message may still be available.

Choose which accounts you can view

Posts from other accounts will be hidden or blocked. If an account can't be identified, its posts stay blocked until an approved account is recognized. Messaging isn't restricted by these account lists.

Enter one @username or Instagram profile link per line, up to 200 accounts in each list. Account names alone won't work. Blocked accounts take priority over allowed accounts. These controls depend on Instagram identifying the account on the page.
